Symposium on AI Frontiers

Innovations, Challenges & Applications

Date: 29th April 2025 | Mode: Hybrid

Symposium Overview

The rapid advancements in AI are shaping the future of industries, society, and research. As AI continues to revolutionize multiple domains, it is crucial to explore its potential, address challenges, and uncover new frontiers.

This symposium provides a global platform for young investigators to present their work, engage in meaningful discussions, and collaborate on pioneering ideas in AI.

Call for Submissions – Sub-Themes

AI for Sustainable Development

Climate modeling, renewable energy, water management

Ethical AI

Bias, fairness, governance, and policies

AI in Healthcare

Diagnostics, drug discovery, mental health

Generative AI

AI in art, music, and literature

Explainable AI

Trust, transparency, and human-AI collaboration

AI for Business

Finance, automation, and predictive analytics
