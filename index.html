About Symposium

The rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI) are shaping the future of industries, society, and research. As AI continues to revolutionize multiple domains, it is crucial to explore its potential, address challenges, and uncover new frontiers. The "Exploring Artificial Intelligence Frontiers Innovations Challenges and Applications" symposium aims to bring together young investigators from different universities across the globe to put their understanding and review about the recent development in the domain of AI.
This symposium provides a global platform for young investigators to present their work, engage in meaningful discussions, and collaborate on pioneering ideas in AI.

Call for Submissions – Sub-Themes

1. AI for Sustainable Development

  • AI-driven climate modeling
  • Smart grids and renewable energy optimization
  • AI for sustainable agriculture and water management

2. Ethical and Responsible AI

  • Bias, fairness, and transparency in AI models
  • AI governance, regulations, and policies
  • Societal impact of AI

3. AI in Healthcare and Biomedicine

  • AI-powered diagnostics and medical imaging
  • Drug discovery and personalized medicine
  • AI for mental health and well-being

4. Generative AI and Creativity

  • AI in art, music, and literature
  • Large language models and synthetic media
  • Deepfake detection and ethical implications

5. Explainable and Interpretable AI

  • Methods for AI model interpretability
  • Human-AI collaboration and decision-making
  • Trustworthy AI frameworks

6. Edge AI and IoT Integration

  • AI on resource-constrained devices
  • Security and privacy in AI-enabled IoT
  • Real-time AI decision-making

7. AI for Business and Industry 4.0

  • AI-driven automation and robotics
  • AI in finance, supply chain, and logistics
  • Predictive analytics for industry applications

8. Neuro-symbolic and Hybrid AI

  • Combining symbolic reasoning with deep learning
  • Hybrid AI models for cognitive computing
  • AI-driven knowledge representation

9. AI in Education and E-Learning

  • Personalized learning and intelligent tutoring
  • AI for automated grading and assessment
  • AI-powered virtual learning environments

10. Quantum AI and Next-Generation Computing

  • AI algorithms for quantum computing
  • Quantum-enhanced machine learning
  • Future directions in AI and quantum synergy
